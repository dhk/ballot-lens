# Proposed SF pilot and editorial review rubric

Status: **draft for human decision**. No pilot selection, reviewer authorization, finding, or source-rights approval is made by this document. Requirements: R-02, D-02/D-03, E-01–E-03, S-02, T-05. Companion: [source inventory](../inventory/sf-2026-11-03.md).

## Proposed pilot

Retain B, G, and H as candidates for an initial contrasting slice: institutional design, access policy, and taxation. These are testing needs, not predictions of outcomes or vote recommendations. Their qualification is checked against the official inventory; suitability and selection still need the product/editorial owner's approval.

Do not assume an amendment's substantive effect or treat a pamphlet passage as current until an editor compares versions. D, outside the proposed pilot, has an argument challenge and departmental response that the inventory must retain.

## Proposed review procedure

1. Pin the election, measure, source URL, exact retrieved bytes/hash and publication/effective dates where known. Separate dates stated by the publisher from HTTP and PDF metadata. Record reuse permissions independently; unknown rights block redistribution.
2. Classify each candidate assertion as official text, fiscal estimate, advocacy argument, contextual evidence, or editorial synthesis. Publication in the official pamphlet authenticates publication, not the truth of an advocacy claim.
3. Record a bounded assertion and scope, exact source revision and excerpt/page locator, counterevidence, uncertainty, and any effective timing or implementation dependency. Split mixed assertions into reviewable claims. Avoid numerical impact estimates unsupported by the source.
4. An assigned human reviews source fidelity, locator resolution, scope, omitted qualifications, counterevidence, and uncertainty. Record disagreements explicitly. Model extraction creates candidates only. No generated explanation promotes its own findings.
5. An authorized human records actor, timestamp, action and reason for draft → in_review → approved/rejected. Approval requires a named authorized actor; the project must define that authority before real editorial writes exist. Terminal records are not revived; corrections create linked revisions.
6. Before publication, resolve source amendments/challenges and rights, verify approved provenance/citations, and label every measure's coverage honestly. Newly changed source dependencies become stale and require re-review; prior release content remains reproducible.

## Proposed acceptance checklist

- The assertion is supported within the cited scope and does not turn a conditional estimate into a fact.
- Citation locators resolve to the pinned original/extraction version, with correct page-number conventions.
- Opposing evidence and uncertainty are visible; missing evidence results in a limitation rather than fabricated certainty.
- No organization preference, personal circumstance, composite score, or vote recommendation changes the evidence finding.
- No draft, rejected, superseded, retracted, or stale revision appears as a current approved finding.
- Distribution rights, editorial approval, technical validation and public-release approval are separate checks.

## Amendments, corrections and incomplete captures

The qualification page lists an amended B Controller statement, a corrected H rebuttal, and a D argument challenge/department response. These labels flag mandatory comparison before treating dependent analysis as current; this inventory does not adjudicate their contents or discard earlier versions. All linked revisions are retained separately in the metadata.

Of 119 linked documents, 113 originals were captured and hashed; six paid-argument originals exceed the 32 MiB inspection bound and remain metadata-only (one reports about 159 MB). Their URLs and available HTTP metadata are recorded as metadata-only, with no invented hash or implied snapshot. The consolidated pamphlet remains a separate source version and cannot silently substitute for an amended individual document. Successful captures do not establish editorial approval or redistribution rights. No source is inferred missing merely because a metadata-only capture lacks bytes.
